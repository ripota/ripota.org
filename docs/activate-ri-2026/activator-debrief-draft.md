# Activator debrief survey draft

**Status: proposed for user review.** This is copy for a small Google Form; no
Form has been created. A future event and its dates are not yet confirmed.

## Introduction

Thanks for being part of Activate All RI. We'd appreciate a few minutes of
feedback to help us learn from the weekend and inform any future event. Share
as much or as little as you like; contact details are optional.

## Questions

| Question | Google Forms response type | Required? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overall, how was your experience activating during Activate All RI? | Multiple choice: Very good; Good; Neither good nor poor; Poor; Very poor | Yes |
| What worked well for you? | Paragraph | No |
| What, if anything, made participating difficult? | Paragraph. Help text: For example: planning, park access, information, coordination, or operating. | No |
| If we hold another event, what is the one thing you would most like us to change or add? | Paragraph | No |
| Would you consider activating in another event like this? | Multiple choice: Yes; Maybe; No | No |
| Callsign and email, if you would like an organizer to follow up. | Short answer | No |

## Submission confirmation

Thanks for sharing your experience—and for helping put Rhode Island parks on
the air.

## Proposed form setup

- Aim for about three minutes; require only the first question.
- Leave automatic email collection off so contact details remain optional.
- Do not require a Google sign-in or ask participants to re-enter QSO totals or
  their itinerary. Existing event evidence can supply the operational counts.
- Keep responses available to organizers; review the question wording before
  creating or sharing the Form.
