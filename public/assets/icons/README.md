# Map service icons

These unmodified service icons identify links to their respective Maps apps.
The site displays them at 24 × 24 pixels inside 44 × 44 pixel links.

- `google-maps.png`: [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/about/) favicon,
  [48 × 48 PNG source](https://www.gstatic.com/marketing-cms/assets/images/81/e4/1bdb808b40a28cc70ac24bf5e6fa/google-maps-favicon.png=s48).
- `apple-maps.png`: [Apple Maps](https://maps.apple.com/) app icon,
  [120 × 120 PNG source](https://maps.apple.com/static/maps-app-web-client/images/maps-app-icon-120x120.png).

Retrieved September 12, 2026. The icons remain the property of their owners.
